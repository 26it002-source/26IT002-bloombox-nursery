About BloomBox Nursery

Located in the heart of Vallabh Vidyanagar, BloomBox Nursery provides vibrant, healthy plants tailored for student hostels, residences, and offices. We specialize in air-purifying indoor greenery, seasonal blooms, and low-maintenance succulents.

We pride ourselves on offering doorstep delivery across Vallabh Vidyanagar and Vidyanagar student zones, along with personalized guidance for plant care.
